## What banks do not control

If banks create money, it may sound as if they can lend without limit.

They cannot.

Banks remain constrained by capital requirements, liquidity requirements, regulation, creditworthy borrowers, and central-bank policy rates.

## Housing as a credit machine

Imagine a city with 100 houses. If banks become willing to issue larger mortgages, buyers can bid more for the same homes. The result is often higher prices rather than more housing.

## A common criticism

Some economists argue that the importance of bank money creation can be overstated because central banks, capital markets, and non-bank financial institutions also shape credit creation.
