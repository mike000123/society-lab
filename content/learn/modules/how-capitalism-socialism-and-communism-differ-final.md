## Comparing the three systems

| Question | Capitalism | Socialism | Communism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Mostly private | Mixed/public/cooperative | Common ownership |
| Investment decisions | Markets | Markets + planning | Collective planning |
| Income distribution | Market outcomes | More redistribution | Intended classless distribution |

## Why no country is purely capitalist or socialist

Most real economies are hybrids containing both market and public institutions.

## The strongest criticisms of each system

Capitalism: inequality, public-good problems, environmental externalities.

Socialism: bureaucracy, weaker innovation incentives, concentration of power.

Communism: planning difficulties, incentive problems, historical authoritarian outcomes.
