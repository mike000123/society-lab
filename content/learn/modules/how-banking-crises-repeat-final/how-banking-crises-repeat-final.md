## Why crises often seem rational at the time

Participants in credit booms are rarely irrational. During the housing boom before 2008, defaults were low, house prices had risen for years, and banks appeared profitable.

## The recurring pattern across centuries

1637 — Dutch Tulip Mania
1720 — South Sea Bubble
1873 — Long Depression
1929 — Great Depression
1980s — Savings and Loan Crisis
2008 — Global Financial Crisis
2023 — Silicon Valley Bank

## Why stability creates instability

Hyman Minsky argued that long periods of stability encourage greater risk-taking, eventually making the system fragile.
